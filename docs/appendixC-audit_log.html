Substantive findings independent of code quality

Three findings from the audit are independent of any implementation issue and remain valid regardless of pipeline-level changes:

  1. Endogeneity is not detected by the Wooldridge-style test for C-section in any of the four BDHS waves (Chapter 8). The residual-coefficient p-value exceeds 0.19 in all four waves. This finding comes from non-bootstrap point estimation; the cluster-bootstrap fixes (A4, A6) do not change it. The substantive implication is that the RBVP machinery may have been deployed to correct a problem the data does not show.

  2. The exclusion restriction is decisively violated by the cross-instrument placebo test (Chapter 9). 8/8 cells reject at p < 10⁻⁷; the magnitudes are nowhere near borderline. This is again a non-bootstrap, point-level finding.

  3. The bivariate normality assumption fails the copula sensitivity check (Chapter 10). 5/8 cells flip sign across Gaussian, Frank, and Clayton families.

These three findings, in combination, make the case for the brief’s “discard the causal claim and pivot” conclusion. None of them depends on the bootstrap-CI machinery or the cluster-resampling fix — they are diagnostic-level findings that survive whatever further methodological pass one might apply.
